Soda is the default. It doesn't have to be.

For a lot of us, the drink we reach for is the one that is already cold and already there. Most of the time we don't really choose it.

Fruithaus exists to give you something else to reach for: drinks made from fruit and nuts, from Benin City, that you can pick on purpose.

We are not here to tell anyone what to stop drinking. We just want to put another good option on the table, and to be straightforward with you about what is in the bottle.

How to Order

  1. 1

    Choose your drinks

    Set the quantity for each drink and add it to your order. Mix all four if you like.

  2. 2

    Review and continue

    Check your order and total, then continue to WhatsApp. Your message is written for you.

  3. 3

    Confirm with us

    Send the message. We confirm availability, delivery or pickup, and payment with you there.

Sending the WhatsApp message does not place or pay for your order. It is only confirmed once we reply to you.
